About / Help

About this system

Calvin Airlines Inc. is a front-end prototype of a flight ticketing system. It lets passengers choose a flight, select seats from a seat map, see the total cost and receive a ticket confirmation with a reference number. It is built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript only; bookings are stored in your browser using localStorage.

How to use it

  1. Browse flights: open Flights, set your travel date and filter by route. Press Select flight.
  2. Book a ticket: on Book a Ticket, confirm the flight and date, set the number of passengers, tap that many seats, then fill in the names and contact details.
  3. Confirm: choose a simulated payment method, tick the demonstration notice and press Confirm booking. Your e-ticket with its reference number appears.
  4. Manage: in My Bookings you can search, view a ticket, edit contact/passenger details or status, cancel (seats are released) or delete a booking.

Good to know

  • Prices are in Philippine pesos (₱). Total = seat fares + 12% VAT + ₱150 booking fee per ticket. Business seats (rows 1–3) cost twice the base fare.
  • Seats already reserved in your saved bookings cannot be selected again. Cancelling a booking releases its seats.
  • Travel dates in the past, and flights that already departed today, cannot be booked.
  • Simulated features: payments, seat availability (based only on this browser's records) and email/SMS notifications.
  • Clearing your browser data removes saved bookings. Use Export JSON in My Bookings to keep a backup.
